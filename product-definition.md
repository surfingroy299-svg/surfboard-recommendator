# product-definition.md

## 1. Problem

Intermediate surfers can feel how a surfboard performs (e.g. stability, speed, control) but cannot translate those sensations into a concrete board choice.

They struggle to map their experience, ability, and goals into specific parameters like board type, size, and model. As a result, they rely on external opinions (shop sellers, friends, coaches), receive inconsistent or biased advice, and lack confidence in their decision.

This often leads to suboptimal board choices and slowed progression.

---

## 2. Target User

Intermediate surfers:
- 1–5 years of experience  
- Surfing 1–3 times per week  
- Past their first board  
- Looking to upgrade or refine their setup  

They:
- Can describe how a board feels, but lack technical knowledge  
- Are unsure what to choose next  
- Depend on others for recommendations  
- Want to improve their surfing and make better decisions  

---

## 3. Core Value Proposition

Help intermediate surfers translate their experience, ability, and goals into a clear surfboard choice - without relying on sellers or friends, and with a clear understanding of why.

---

## 4. Product Output

Each user receives:

- **Board category**  
  (e.g. groveler, fish, shortboard, step-up)

- **Size recommendation (personalized)**  
  Volume range (liters) and length range based on the user’s profile

- **2–3 board models**  
  Specific, curated recommendations

- **Explanation**
  - Why this setup fits the user  
  - One key advantage per model  
  - One key trade-off per model  

---

## 5. Scope (V1 only)

- Guided decision-making experience based on user input  
- Personalized surfboard recommendation  
- Clear, non-technical explanations  
- Limited, curated output (no browsing)  
- System presents only a small set of recommended options (no open exploration or catalog access)  

---

## 6. Out of Scope (V1)

- No inventory or store integration  
- No purchasing flow  
- No price comparison or availability  
- No wishlist or saving  
- No open catalog browsing  
- No advanced comparison tables  

---

## 7. Success Criteria

A successful experience is defined by:

- Users reach a clear recommendation  
- Users understand why the recommendation fits them  
- Users feel the recommendation matches their real surfing experience  
- Users feel confident making a decision without external help  
- Low drop-off during the decision flow  

---

## 8. Design Principle

**Explainability over accuracy**  
A good recommendation must be understood by the user, not just correct.
