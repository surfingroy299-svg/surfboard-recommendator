# Shared Prototype Scenario — the 21 steps

Identical scenario in all three directions (fair comparison). Data:
`surfer.json` + `recommendation.json`. Primary set: Better Everyday
(Comfortable Choice) · G Skate (Best Match) · Two Happy (Progression Option).
Pool: Dumpster Diver 2, Fever.

1. **Enter** the recommendation experience (from the launcher; each direction
   has its own entry moment).
2. **See three boards together** with their roles.
3. **Understand each board's role** (role + one-line why visible or one
   action away).
4. **Manipulate boards / navigate the space** (per-direction interaction).
5. **Select** G Skate.
6. **Bring it into focus.**
7. **Inspect from relevant angles** (deck / bottom / rail / tail per
   direction's inspection model).
8. **Understand why recommended** (why + evidence).
9. **See its main advantage.**
10. **See its main trade-off.**
11. **Select a second board** (Better Everyday).
12. **Enter comparison mode.**
13. **Compare the complete boards** (whole-form comparison at shared scale).
14. **Compare ≥1 physical characteristic** (outline/width/length/volume).
15. **Compare ≥1 performance dimension** (trait delta, qualitative).
16. **Exit comparison** without losing orientation.
17. **Reject** Two Happy.
18. **Lock** G Skate (and optionally Better Everyday).
19. **Replace only the rejected board** → Fever arrives (per replacement
    table) with its reason.
20. **Understand why the replacement differs** (reason + delta tags; Fever's
    honest weak condition-fit surfaces).
21. **Continue exploring** the updated set (focus/inspect/compare again; refine
    chips available).

Uncertainty presentation (REAL rule): one flag (`insufficient_data`) → each
direction notes the missing-dimensions uncertainty once, visibly, without
undermining the recommendation. Data gaps (tail/rails/concave unknowns,
requirement vocabulary) render as explicit "unknown — we don't guess" marks.
