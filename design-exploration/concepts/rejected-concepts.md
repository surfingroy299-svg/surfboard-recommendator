# Rejected Concepts

| Seed | Rejected because |
|---|---|
| **S3 — The Lineup** | Credible water is the single most expensive real-time effect available and cheap water is instantly ugly (feasibility 2, performance 2); water occludes the bottom half of every board — the region (rails, fins, bottom contour) comparison needs most; contextual beauty over decision value. The "in your waves" *framing sentence* survives as copy, not as environment. |
| **S5 — The Tradeoff Field** | The metaphor itself needs a tutorial (explainability 2); worse, board positions in a continuous field assert a precision of ranking that the product's coarse signals (1–5 traits, qualitative directions) cannot support — structural fake-precision, architecture fit 2. |
| **S6 — The Wave Section** | Rejected on honesty at the metaphor level: placing boards at wave positions is a hydrodynamic performance claim; the project data supports condition *tags*, not wave-position performance. No amount of labeling fixes a core metaphor that overclaims. |
| **S7 — The X-Ray Table** | Strong but narrow: a comparison mode, not a product experience (no presence, roles, or why-layer). Absorbed into Direction 02 as its overlay-comparison mode. |
| **S8 — The Turntable Gallery** | The default 3D-product-viewer pattern (anti-principle A4 verbatim); distinctiveness 1. Nothing here a stock R3F demo doesn't already do. |
| **S9 — The Shaper's Walkthrough** | Authored linearity cannot host the nonlinear lock/reject/replace loop that Phases 10–12 require (A7). Its best moment — the guided first-board walkthrough with camera-synced explanation — is harvested into Direction 01's entry sequence. |
| **S10 — The Gear Locker** | Slots + tooltips + stat chips is a dashboard in costume; boards become icons instead of the interface, violating the brief's core demand. Its delta-direction language (qualitative, accessibility-safe) is harvested into Directions 02 and 03. |

Common rejection themes: environmental spectacle without decision value
(S3, S6), abstraction demanding its own tutorial (S5), conventionality in
disguise (S8, S10), and structural dishonesty no labeling can repair (S5, S6).
