# Direction 02 — The Measure Room — Critical Assessment

- **Main strength:** the highest decision-per-pixel of the three: aligned
  overlay with divergence fringes, direction-arrow delta rail, and the
  waterline ruler with local Δ + consequence line — comparison that answers
  "which and why" fastest. Fully accessible by construction (all DOM/SVG).
- **Main weakness:** the least spectacular entry moment; its calm can read as
  austere before the user touches the overlay. The profile (rocker) view is
  the thinnest artifact — deliberately dashed as a sketch, but visually modest.
- **Main UX risk:** instrument aesthetics may intimidate the least technical
  users the product serves; consequence sentences carry the burden of warmth.
- **Main technical risk:** effectively none — no WebGL, no fallback anxiety;
  it IS the fallback tier proof.
- **Risk of gimmick:** the lowest of the three; nothing here is decoration.
- **Honest trade-off:** it never gives the object itself — you read the board,
  you never *hold* it. Physical presence is traded for legibility.
- **Changes by round:** R1 established the table + overlay; R2 de-carded the
  plates (film-on-table + drafting title blocks), scaled everything up,
  clamped the ruler to measured width; R3 enlarged the overlay stage.
- **Verification note:** complete 21-step scenario driven live in-session
  (focus, overlay, lock stamp, reject→Fever with revision note, undo).
