# Direction 02 — The Measure Room

**One sentence:** A quiet instrument room where your three boards exist as
living measured drawings on a great light-table — and every difference between
them draws itself, then tells you what it means.

- **Product metaphor:** the drafting archive / instrument room — where shapers'
  actual language (outline, profile, section) becomes something an
  intermediate can finally read.
- **Intended emotional response:** calm command. The relief of seeing a
  confusing choice become legible. Precision that feels *kind*, not clinical.
- **Why it fits the product:** the product's honesty rules (ranges not points,
  evidence not hype, unknowns stay unknown) *are* a drafting sensibility. This
  direction turns the product's epistemology into its aesthetic.
- **Why it improves decision-making:** aligned orthographic silhouettes with
  divergence shading are the highest-information comparison the data honestly
  supports (P13); every drawn measurement carries a consequence sentence
  (P12); qualitative delta chips give direction-of-difference at a glance
  (P11).
- **Why it is not conventional ecommerce:** no photography, no cards, no grid
  — the boards are drawings that behave like objects; the interface is a
  table, not a page. A spec sheet is what this direction *replaces*.
- **3D value check (step 6):** deliberately *controlled* spatiality — depth,
  plate layering, and coordinated view flips give orientation and object
  presence, while orthographic truth does the comparing. This direction is
  the argument that full free 3D is not automatically the best decision tool
  — its identity depends on that restraint.
- **Territory:** calm / precise / controlled.
- **Harvested:** S7's x-ray overlay is this direction's comparison mode; S10's
  qualitative delta chips are its difference language.
