# Direction 03 — The Quiver Deck

**One sentence:** Your recommended quiver as a bold, springy game table —
grab a board, spin it in your hands, clamp the ones you love, eject the one
you don't, and watch the system deal you a better card with its reasons
printed on the tag.

- **Product metaphor:** the deck/table — Coolors made spatial. Decision as
  confident play: locking, ejecting, and re-dealing are the primary verbs.
- **Intended emotional response:** energy and agency. The grin of flicking a
  board into a spin; zero fear of rejecting anything (P3); iteration feels
  like appetite, not anxiety.
- **Why it fits the product:** the product's real loop *is* iterate-within-a-
  curated-set (small set, replace only what fails you). This direction makes
  the loop the hero instead of burying it behind inspection.
- **Why it improves decision-making:** frictionless rejection produces more
  honest choices (users keep what survived comparison, not what they were
  afraid to lose); every swap carries a printed reason and a delta tag
  (A2 honored: never random); physical spin-to-inspect makes form tangible
  (P4) without camera skills.
- **Why it is not conventional ecommerce:** boards are game pieces with mass,
  not thumbnails; there is no list to scroll, no cart psychology; rejection
  is celebrated with a spring instead of hidden in a heart icon.
- **3D value check (step 6):** the physical feel *is* the decision aid —
  snap-spin between deck/bottom/rail is faster than any camera UI, and mass
  feedback keeps the playfulness attached to the object being judged. Guard
  rails: every fling snaps to a legible face (A1 — play never gates
  information).
- **Territory:** playful / energetic / experimental.
- **Harvested:** S10's delta language as oversized face-off tags.
