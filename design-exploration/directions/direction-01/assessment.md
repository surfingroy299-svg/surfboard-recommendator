# Direction 01 — The Shaping Bay — Critical Assessment

- **Main strength:** the metaphor does real explanatory work — region-anchored
  pencil notes + a chalkboard ledger stage the product's evidence as
  craftsman expertise, and the lamp-pool art direction makes the room feel
  authored, not stock-3D.
- **Main weakness:** the procedural blank is the weakest board form of the
  three at close range (nose/rail read is sketch-grade). It is honest and
  labeled, but a chosen-direction build should exercise the documented asset
  contingency (Blender-refined blank or licensed GLB).
- **Main UX risk:** station-based cameras depend on well-chosen stations;
  users who expect free orbit may feel gently railed.
- **Main technical risk:** none unusual — standard R3F; the station/damp rig
  is simple and portable.
- **Risk of gimmick:** low-moderate. The room is set dressing; if the notes
  and chalkboard were removed it would become decoration — they are the
  direction's spine and must stay primary.
- **Honest trade-off:** warmth and trust cost information density — one board
  in focus at a time; comparison shows two boards but fewer numbers than the
  Measure Room.
- **Changes by round:** R1 fixed inverted normals; R2 rebuilt geometry,
  reframed all stations, added lamp-pool lighting, floor-standing rack;
  R3 fixed the compare staging (lateral-high camera; both stands read) and
  decluttered hotspots during compare.
- **Verification note:** full scenario driven via keyboard paths + deep-link
  states in this session; pointer-drag rotation exercised through its
  keyboard/station equivalents and code review, not a physical pointer.
