# Direction 01 — Visual Model

Developed independently for this direction (no shared tokens across
directions during exploration).

- **Palette:** warm neutrals — plywood `#C89B6C`, warm plaster `#EFE6D8`,
  shadow umber `#4A3B2C`; foam-white boards `#F4F1E8`; accent: worn teal
  `#2E6E65` (tags, checks); chalk `#F8F5EC` on slate `#2F3230`. Text ink
  `#2A241C` on plaster (contrast-checked in build).
- **Typography:** Fraunces (warm, slightly soft serif) for headings/role tags;
  Inter for body/chalkboard data; chalk annotations in Fraunces italic — no
  novelty handwriting font (credibility over cosplay).
- **Environment:** a shallow room — plaster walls, one plywood wall, floor
  with foam-dust gradient; racks stage the set; shaping stands center; the
  chalkboard wall is the data surface. Sparse: nothing in the room that isn't
  an interface.
- **Background/depth:** warm gradient falloff into soft darkness beyond the
  lamp's reach; depth cues from light, not fog.
- **Lighting:** key = work lamp, warm (3200K feel), raking ~30° across the
  board to reveal form; fill = cool ambient bounce; single soft contact
  shadow under stands. Light *is* the inspection tool (rail/foil reads come
  from the raking highlight, labeled sketch-level).
- **Materials:** matte foam with subtle noise; pencil-line stringer; no gloss,
  no showroom clearcoat (approximation honesty: it reads as a *shaped blank*,
  not a finished retail board — which is exactly the labeled fidelity level).
- **Controls:** minimal DOM — station names top-left, rack tags, chalkboard.
  Buttons look like taped labels/stamped tags, never glass cards.
- **Motion language:** slow confident glides 450–650ms ease-out (camera),
  damped board rotation, chalk notes write in 200ms; nothing bounces. Reduced
  motion: crossfades + stepped faces.
- **DOM ↔ boards relationship:** the scene is the stage, the chalkboard (DOM)
  is the ledger; pencil notes are DOM anchored to 3D points. UI supports the
  board — the board never sits inside a card.
